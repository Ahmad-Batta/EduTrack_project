About EduTrack

Welcome to EduTrack, the ultimate academic workspace designed to bridge the gap between instructors and students. Our mission is to simplify course management, assignment tracking, and daily educational tasks through a clean, modern, and intuitive interface.

Whether you are an instructor managing multiple classes and assignments or a student keeping up with deadlines, EduTrack provides the robust tools you need—from multi-tenancy data isolation to real-time academic tracking—all in one secure place.
